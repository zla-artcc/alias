~~~~~~~~~~~~~~T A B L E   O F   C O N T E N T S~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Copy and paste any line (minus tabs) into CTRL-F to navigate to that line.
Lines starting with a [.] period indicate an alias command and the output of that command. For exmaple:
.kramerwashere .msg 845421_OBS Hey Kramer, how are you?
When you type ".kramerwashere" and hit enter in the chat window, it will send the ".msg" command as-written.
